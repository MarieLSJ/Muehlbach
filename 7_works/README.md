# Bibliographic information about the seven works by Luise Mühlbach that Otto Janke reissued under new titles from 1857 to 1860, with translations and lending libraries holdings
**Collected by Marie Léger-St-Jean, October 2026**

## Contents of the directory

- [7_reissued_works.csv](7_reissued_works.csv)
- [7_reissued_works.ods](7_reissued_works.ods)

## Abstract
This table contains bibliographical information pertaining to a group of seven works by Luise Mühlbach, pen name of Clara Mundt (1814–1873), _née_ Müller, that Otto Janke (1818–1887) reissued from 1857 to 1860 under new titles. It contains for each bibliographical information on the first edition and the revised second edition; the first apparent differences between the two; bibliographical information about Dutch translations and, where application, Danish, British, and American translations; and, finally, indication of which German edition, if any, was available in seven different German-language _Leihbibliotheken_ (lending libraries).

We produced this table as part of the book historical research supporting the following article:

>Elzen, Sophie van den, and Marie Léger-St-Jean. February 2027. “Censorship and the Historical Novel: Revisiting and Revising 1848 in Luise Mühlbach’s Reworking of _Aphra Behn_.” _boundary 2: An International Journal of Literature and Culture_ 54 (1). [https://read.dukeupress.edu/boundary-2](https://read.dukeupress.edu/boundary-2).

### Differences between the editions	
This column records the first apparent differences between the first and the revised editions. It is designed to find which edition served as source text for a translation. It also points to the significance of the edition statements since Mühlbach made more extensive revisions in the first three "_Zweite, **neu** umgearbeitete Ausgabe_" and the 1859 "_Zweite **völlig** umgearbeitete Ausgabe_" than in the three 1860 "_Zweite umgearbeitete Ausgabe_".

### Presence of each edition in lending libraries	
The seven columns in this section of the table record which (German) edition, if any, was available in different German-language _Leihbibliotheken_ (lending libraries). Their presence is ascertained in five cases through the digital surrogates of catalogs and in two cases by the holdings of an institutional library which bought the collection in the twentieth century.

>[!NOTE] "1" indicates the presence of only the first edition; "2", that of only the revised edition; and "1,2" the presence of both.
