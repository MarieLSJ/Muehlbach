# Muehlbach
Datasets about the seven works by Luise Mühlbach that Otto Janke reissued under new titles from 1857 to 1860, centred on *Aphra Behn. Roman* (Berlin: M. Simion, 1849) and *Karl der Zweite und sein Hof. Historischer Roman* (Berlin: Otto Janke, 1859). They support an article by Sophie van den Elzen and Marie Léger-St-Jean forthcoming in Feb 2027.
