## Abstract
Publisher Otto Janke reissued seven works by Luise Mühlbach under new titles from 1857 to 1860 and billed them second “_bearbeitete Ausgabe[n]_” (reworked editions). Marie Léger-St-Jean gathered bibliographical information about the seven pair of novels in what has become data package [`7_works`](../7_works) in this repository. She identified that the last three works to be reissued, all in 1860, were less revised than the previous ones. Sophie van den Elzen confirmed it by skimming through the accompanying collations this data package named `1860_ZuA` for “_Zweite bearbeitete Ausgabe_” (ZuA).

We produced these collations as part of the book historical research supporting the following article:
>Elzen, Sophie van den, and Marie Léger-St-Jean. February 2027. “Censorship and the Historical Novel: Revisiting and Revising 1848 in Luise Mühlbach’s Reworking of _Aphra Behn_.” _boundary 2: An International Journal of Literature and Culture_ 54 (1). [https://read.dukeupress.edu/boundary-2](https://read.dukeupress.edu/boundary-2).

## Investigation
As part of her book historical research into the set of seven works by Luise Mühlbach reissued as second editions by Otto Janke, Marie tracked their translations into Dutch, Danish, and English (both in the United Kingdom and in the United States). To help ascertain which edition had been translated —the first or the second, revised edition— Marie noted for each work in the table contained in `7_works` the first apparent differences between the two editions (column `Differences between the editions`). Differences were immediately apparent as early as the first page for the first four revised editions. She had to look further in the text before she could find significant differences that would turn up in a translation for the last three revised editions, all published in 1860.

Marie also noticed different wordings for the edition statements on the title pages of Otto Janke’s second, revised editions: 
- “_Zweite, neu bearbeitete Ausgabe_” (first three);
- “_Zweite völlig umgearbeitete Ausgabe_” (middle one);
- “_Zweite bearbeitete Ausgabe_” (last three).

Janke thus described on their title pages the same subset of three works with harder to find differences with the shorter “_Zweite bearbeitete Ausgabe_”.

Marie collated the first volumes of all three works using the digital tool Locate, Explore, Retrace and Apprehend complex text variants, or [LERA](https://lera.uzi.uni-halle.de/), developed by Marcus Pöckelmann and his colleagues, to ensure that she had not missed any other differences early in the text. Sophie, who had close read the collation of _Aphra Behn_ (1849) and _Karl der Zweite und sein Hof_ (1859), skimmed through the new collations and confirmed that little had been revised in comparison.

## Initial findings
We infer that the precise wording of publisher Otto Janke’s edition statements on the title pages of his second, revised editions indicates the latter’s level of reworking.
